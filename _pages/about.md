I am currently a Post Doctoral Researcher at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology in Germany, working on data-driven modeling and meta-modeling in Software Engineering.  Within the CONVIDE project, I contribute as a methodologist in the development of the Virtual Single Underlying Model (VSUM) and its metamodel (VSUMM). My doctoral research at Tallinn University of Technology (TalTech), Estonia, focused on unifying association rule mining, OLAP, and statistical reasoning, with a particular emphasis on numerical association rule mining and the discretization of numerical attributes in scientific knowledge discovery.

Previously, I worked as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Raj Kumar Goel Institute of Technology, Ghaziabad, India. Over the course of fifteen years in academia, I have gained extensive teaching and mentoring experience, covering a broad range of courses including Data Science, Data Mining, Database Systems, and Machine Learning, while mentoring students at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

My research is interdisciplinary and collaborative in nature, combining data mining, modeling, and explainable AI to advance both theoretical foundations and practical applications. I am deeply committed to fostering academic excellence, driving innovation in research and pedagogy, and building strong international collaborations that bridge the gap between research and teaching.
